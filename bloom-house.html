FAQ

Quick answers

How does pricing work?
Bloom House is $90 per hour with a 4 hour minimum. Cleaning service is an optional $100 add on.
Where exactly is Bloom House?
It is a private home in Orlando, FL. The exact address is shared with you once your booking is confirmed.
What is the cancellation policy?
You get a full refund if you cancel within 24 hours of confirmation, as long as the event is more than 48 hours away. Any security deposit is returned 48 hours after your event.
Can I bring food, a cake and decorations?
Yes. You can use the kitchen and BBQ area, and the host can also help with decorations, tableware and light catering on request.
Is there a bathroom and parking?
Guests can use an indoor bathroom from the covered porch. There are 25 parking spots on the property plus street parking nearby.
